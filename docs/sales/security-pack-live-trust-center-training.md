# Live Trust Center–Driven Sales Enablement

Anchor answers to Trust Center pages; avoid ad-hoc explanations or promises.

## Scenarios and Scripts

### Security Review Call

- **Reference page:** Trust Center (Overview, Access Control, Approvals, Audit Integrity).
- **Say:** “Let’s walk through the Trust Center page for how access, approvals, and audit logging work. This is the authoritative description.”
- **Do NOT add:** No SLAs, no guarantees, no custom controls, no paraphrasing beyond the page.

### Procurement Questionnaire Walkthrough

- **Reference page:** Trust Center (Transparency/Non-Guarantees) + final procurement questionnaire.
- **Say:** “Your questionnaire answers match what’s in our Trust Center. For detailed Q&A, here’s the final questionnaire text we use.”
- **Do NOT add:** No pricing, no custom formats, no promises of faster delivery.

### Legal Escalation

- **Reference page:** Trust Center (Explicit Non-Guarantees) + Positioning/contract notes (internal).
- **Say:** “Our public position is in the Trust Center; entitlements are contract-controlled and not self-service. We don’t offer SLAs or certification guarantees.”
- **Do NOT add:** No new terms, no toggle/self-service offers, no implied amendments.

### Audit Follow-Up Email

- **Reference page:** Trust Center (Audit & Evidence Integrity; Automation) + SOC-2 readiness (if requested).
- **Email template:**  
  Subject: Security Pack — Audit Follow-Up  
  Body: “Per our conversation, here is the Trust Center section covering audit logging, evidence handling, and automation controls. Access is contract-enforced, approvals are audited, and logs are append-only. Let me know if you need the standard readiness materials.”
- **Do NOT add:** No certification claims, no timelines, no custom evidence offers.

## AE Certification Checklist

- Uses Trust Center pages live instead of paraphrasing controls.
- Avoids promises (SLAs, approvals, certifications, custom toggles/formats).
- Knows which page to reference for access, approvals, audit integrity, non-guarantees.
- Escalates to questionnaire/compliance packet only when requested; does not improvise.
