# Public Sector Security Review Email Templates (AI Governance)

Formal templates for government and education procurement.

## 1) Evidence Delivery

- **Subject:** AI governance evidence for your review
- **Body:**
  - Hello [Name],
  - Providing AI governance materials for your assessment:
    - Audit CSV Export (workspace-specific)
    - Evidence Bundle (audit CSV + control references)
    - Trust Center and AI Governance Overview
  - Next steps: Please confirm receipt. We can provide additional filters or a walkthrough if required.
  - Regards, [Your Name]

## 2) Governance Clarification

- **Subject:** Clarification on AI governance controls
- **Body:**
  - Hello [Name],
  - Addressing your questions on AI governance:
    - Kill switches: Platform-wide and workspace disablement, enforced server-side
    - Audits: Append-only with integrity hashes; exportable via Audit CSV/Evidence Bundle
    - Rate limits: Workspace/user scoped with retry guidance; alerts on spikes
  - Next steps: We can demonstrate a disable/enable flow in a test workspace or provide a sample audit export.
  - Regards, [Your Name]
