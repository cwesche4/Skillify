Align artifacts to sales stages; avoid over-sharing too early. No pricing or commitments.

## Sales Enablement Guidance

- Lead with governance and auditability; keep entitlements contract-driven in all messaging.
- Use executive diagram and final procurement questionnaire to reduce follow-ups.
- Introduce contract packaging only in Legal/Contracting stage; avoid early legal terms.
- Provide transparency APIs post-close for verification without mutation.
- Green: auto-share; Yellow: share on request; Red: do not share at this stage.

## “Do Not Share Yet” Rules

- No contract language or amendment discussions before Legal/Contracting.
- No timelines, SLAs, pricing, or roadmap at any stage.
- No self-service or SKU framing; always tie to entitlements and contract.

## Who Sends What (Role Ownership)

- AE: Discovery materials (Green), initiate questionnaire sharing (with SE/Compliance alignment).
- SE: Technical validation assets (diagram, summary Q&A), supports procurement packet on request.
- Compliance: Compliance packet, SOC-2 Q&A, readiness scorecard; joins Legal/Contracting conversations.
- Legal: Contract packaging/positioning decision; amendments/exceptions discussion.

## Escalation Triggers

- Requests for exceptions/amendments → escalate to Legal/Compliance.
- Requests for full compliance packet or SOC-2 Q&A → involve Compliance.
- Attempts to negotiate self-service or SKU toggles → escalate to Sales Leadership/Compliance.

## Sales Ops Checklist

- Stage set? Confirm Green assets only unless buyer explicitly requests Yellow. Never share Red.
- Role assigned? AE/SE/Compliance/Legal aligned before sending Yellow assets.
- Compliance packet gated? Only send in Procurement/Legal stages.
- Confirm no pricing/SLAs/roadmap in any shared material.
