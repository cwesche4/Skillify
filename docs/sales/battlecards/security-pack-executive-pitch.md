# Security Pack — Executive Pitch Card

## What It Is (1 paragraph)

Governed, contract-entitled delivery of security evidence for Enterprise customers, with role-gated approvals, server-side enforcement, and append-only audit trails. No self-service toggles, no plan-name shortcuts, and downloads are gated to prevent leakage.

## Why Enterprise Buyers Care

- Reduces procurement/security back-and-forth with a standard, auditable process.
- Delivers audit-ready transparency (timelines, exports) without exposing payloads.
- Lowers legal and operational risk via entitlement controls and concealment (404 for unauthorized/undelivered).

## One-Sentence Differentiation

“Security Pack makes evidence delivery governed, auditable, and contract-enforced—no self-service toggles or mutable history.”
