# Security Pack — Legal / Compliance Card

## Contract Enforcement

- Access is defined by contract entitlements; no plan-name or UI switches.
- Exceptions are time-bound and logged; resolver enforces effective/expiry.

## Entitlements vs SKUs

- Included in Enterprise contracts as a governance module, not sold as a toggle/SKU.
- Mutations occur via contract processes only; system-token protected, not user-driven.

## Explicit Non-Guarantees

- No SLAs or delivery timelines.
- No guaranteed approvals.
- No certification promises; exports are point-in-time snapshots.

## Evidence to Reference

- Positioning decision (final); contract packaging spec; entitlement enforcement summary.
