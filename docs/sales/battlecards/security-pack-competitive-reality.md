# Security Pack — Competitive Reality Card

## Why Enterprise-Included

- Uniform entitlements simplify enforcement and evidence reuse across all Enterprise customers.
- Reduces legal/operational risk and procurement friction versus optional add-ons.

## Why Not Self-Service

- Contract-entitlement enforcement keeps approvals, audits, and downloads governed and auditable.
- Self-service toggles would bypass contract controls and increase audit/legal risk.

## Audit & Legal Risk Reduction

- Append-only audit logs and read-only exports reduce tampering risk.
- 404 concealment for unauthorized/undelivered downloads prevents leakage.
- Scoped automation tokens and idempotent callbacks reduce third-party risk.

## Proof Points to Share

- Entitlement enforcement summary; compliance packet; SOC-2 Q&A; evidence index.
