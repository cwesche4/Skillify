# Enterprise Security Review Email Templates (AI Governance)

Conservative templates for large enterprise and regulated buyers.

## 1) Evidence Package Delivery

- **Subject:** AI governance evidence package for your review
- **Body:**
  - Hi [Name],
  - Providing the requested AI governance materials:
    - Audit CSV Export (workspace-specific)
    - Evidence Bundle (audit CSV + control references)
    - Trust Center and AI Governance Overview
  - Next steps: Please confirm receipt. We can supply additional filters or arrange a walkthrough if needed.
  - Regards, [Your Name]

## 2) SOC-2 / Audit Escalation

- **Subject:** SOC-2 evidence for AI governance controls
- **Body:**
  - Hi [Name],
  - Sharing SOC-2–aligned materials relevant to AI governance:
    - SOC-2 Readiness Packet
    - Control references within the Evidence Bundle
    - Trust Center (public posture)
  - Next steps: Let us know if you require a sample audit export or a short session to review disable/enable procedures.
  - Thank you, [Your Name]

## 3) Follow-Up After Security Review

- **Subject:** Follow-up on AI governance security review
- **Body:**
  - Hi [Name],
  - Following the review, here are the key references:
    - Trust Center
    - Evidence Bundle and Audit CSV export options
    - Runbooks and Tabletop Exercises for incident handling
  - Next steps: We can provide workspace-specific exports or demonstrate controls in a test environment if needed.
  - Regards, [Your Name]
