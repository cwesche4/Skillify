# Security Pack — Sales-Ready Notion Workspace (Enterprise)

Single source of truth for selling and defending Security Pack in Enterprise deals. No pricing, SLAs, roadmap, or internal implementation details exposed.

## Top-Level Pages

1. Sales Home
2. Battlecards
3. Procurement & Security
4. Audit & Compliance

6. “Do Not Say” Guardrails

---

## 1) Sales Home

- **Summary (1 paragraph):** Security Pack = governed, contract-entitled evidence delivery with role-gated approvals, server-side enforcement, append-only audits, and 404 concealment for unauthorized/undelivered access.
- **When to bring it up:** Mention lightly in Discovery; use fully from Security Review onward.
- **Core links:** Battlecards, Procurement Questionnaire (final), Trust Center copy, SOC-2 Readiness, Positioning notes.
- **What NOT to promise:** No pricing, SLAs, roadmap, or self-service claims.

## 2) Battlecards

- **Content mapping:**
  - Competitive positioning / Enterprise-included: `docs/sales/battlecards/security-pack-competitive-reality.md`
  - “Why not self-service?” responses: same card + Trust Center copy.
  - Objection handling: `docs/sales/security-pack-objection-handling.md`
- **When to use:** Live calls, internal prep. Do not forward raw battlecards to customers.
- **What questions it answers:** Competitive “why included,” self-service objections, procurement/security/legal objections.
- **What NOT to promise:** No pricing, no future features, no custom exceptions.

## 3) Procurement & Security

- **Artifacts:** Final procurement questionnaire (`docs/sales/security-pack-procurement-questionnaire-final.md`); Trust Center copy (`docs/trust/security-pack-trust-center.md`).
- **When to send:** When procurement/security blocks or requests detail. Trust Center link is safe for early sharing.
- **Answers:** Access control, approvals, audit integrity, automation, transparency, SOC-2 alignment (no guarantees).
- **What NOT to promise:** No SLAs, no custom formats, no self-service activation.

## 4) Audit & Compliance

- **Artifacts:** SOC-2 readiness scorecard (`docs/compliance/security-pack-soc2-readiness-scorecard.md`); SOC-2 interview simulation (`docs/compliance/security-pack-soc2-interview-simulation.md`); compliance packet overview (`docs/compliance/security-pack-compliance-packet.md`).
- **When to use:** Only when security/audit explicitly asks.
- **Answers:** Evidence sources, readiness status, scripted audit walkthrough.
- **What NOT to promise:** No certification or audit outcomes; no timelines.

## 5) Deal Stage Playbooks

- **Source:** `docs/sales/security-pack-deal-stage-enablement.md`.
- **By stage:**
  - Discovery: Trust Center only; keep high-level.
  - Security Review: Diagram/summary Q&A; questionnaire if requested.
  - Procurement: Send final questionnaire; compliance packet on request; involve SE/Compliance.
  - Late-stage / Audit: SOC-2 Q&A/readiness on request.
  - Legal/Contracting: Positioning & contract notes.
  - Post-signature: Verification instructions (read-only entitlements/timelines).
- **Owner:** AE leads; SE supports; Compliance/Legal joins for packet/contract topics.

## 6) “Do Not Say” Guardrails

- **Red lines:** No pricing/discounts, no SLAs/timelines, no roadmap, no certification promises, no self-service activation claims, no SKU toggles.
- **Common accidental promises:** “We can turn it on anytime,” “We guarantee approval,” “We can customize logs.”
- **Usage:** Keep visible on calls and in prep; overwrite any conflicting language before sharing.

## Access & Permissions

- Sales/SE: edit Sales Home, Battlecards, Playbooks; view Procurement & Security, Audit & Compliance; read Positioning notes.
- Legal/Compliance: view/comment everywhere; manage Positioning notes.
- Engineering: view-only where needed.

## Keep Authoritative

- Link only to approved artifacts listed above; avoid ad-hoc copies.
- Use stage guidance to prevent premature sharing.
- If unsure, default to Trust Center copy and escalate to Compliance for deeper artifacts.
