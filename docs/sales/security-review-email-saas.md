# SaaS Security Review Email Templates (AI Governance)

Neutral, enterprise-ready templates for security and procurement teams.

## 1) Initial Evidence Delivery

- **Subject:** AI governance evidence for your security review
- **Body:**
  - Hi [Name],
  - Sharing the AI governance materials you requested:
    - Audit CSV Export (workspace-specific)
    - Evidence Bundle (audit CSV + control references)
    - Trust Center and AI Governance Overview
  - Next steps: Confirm if you need additional filters or a brief walkthrough of controls.
  - Regards, [Your Name]

## 2) Follow-Up After Questionnaire

- **Subject:** Follow-up on your security questionnaire
- **Body:**
  - Hi [Name],
  - In response to your questionnaire, here are the key references:
    - Trust Center
    - SOC-2 Readiness Packet (AI governance)
    - Evidence Bundle and Audit CSV export options
  - Next steps: We can schedule a short call or provide a sample export from your workspace if helpful.
  - Thanks, [Your Name]

## 3) AI Governance Clarification

- **Subject:** Clarification on AI governance controls
- **Body:**
  - Hi [Name],
  - Addressing your AI governance questions:
    - Kill switches: Global and workspace-level, enforced server-side

  - Regards, [Your Name]
