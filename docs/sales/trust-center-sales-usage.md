# Trust Center Sales Usage Guide — Security Pack

Trust Center is the source of truth; use it to reduce live explanations. No selling language or promises.

## Deal-Stage Mapping

- **Discovery:** Share Trust Center link/copy for high-level governance; do not share compliance packets.
- **Security Review:** Use Trust Center to answer access, approvals, audit integrity, data handling, non-guarantees. Escalate to procurement questionnaire only if requested.
- **Procurement:** Trust Center plus final questionnaire; no additional commitments. Compliance packet only on request via SE/Compliance.
- **Late-Stage Audit:** Trust Center + SOC-2 readiness/Q&A on request; involve Compliance.

## Objections Answered by Trust Center

- Self-service vs contract access.
- Approval enforcement and non-bypass.
- Audit log integrity (append-only, metadata-only).
- Download controls and concealment.
- Expiration of access (contract-bound entitlements).
- Evidence exports (read-only, point-in-time).
- SOC-2 alignment (no guarantees).

## When to Escalate Beyond Trust Center

- Buyer requests detailed questionnaire → share final procurement questionnaire.
- Buyer requests evidence exports/readiness → involve SE/Compliance; share compliance packet/Q&A.
- Contract or exceptions discussion → Legal/Compliance only.

## Do / Don’t

| Do                                                                      | Don’t                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Use Trust Center copy verbatim to answer governance questions.          | Add promises (SLAs, certifications, timelines). |
| Emphasize contract entitlements and append-only audits.                 | Offer self-service activation or SKU toggles.   |
| Redirect deep security asks to procurement questionnaire or Compliance. | Improvise custom controls or formats.           |
| Keep non-guarantees explicit (no guaranteed approvals, no SLAs).        | Suggest future features or roadmap.             |

## Sales Must Never Say

- “We can turn this on via UI or toggle.”
- “We guarantee approval or delivery timelines.”
- “We can customize logs/exports.”
- “We are certified” (only alignment, no promises).
