# Fintech Security Review Email Templates (AI Governance)

Conservative, audit-focused templates for financial services security teams.

## 1) Evidence Delivery (Audit CSV / Evidence Bundle)

- **Subject:** AI governance evidence delivery (audit export enclosed)
- **Body:**
  - Hi [Name],
  - As requested, here are the AI governance artifacts for your review:
    - Audit CSV Export (workspace-specific)
    - Evidence Bundle (includes audit CSV + control references)
    - Trust Center and AI Governance Overview
  - Next steps: Confirm receipt. If you need additional filters or a live walkthrough, we can schedule a short session.
  - Regards, [Your Name]

## 2) SOC-2 / Risk Committee Escalation

- **Subject:** SOC-2 evidence for AI governance controls
- **Body:**
  - Hi [Name],
  - Sharing SOC-2–aligned materials for your risk committee review:
    - SOC-2 Readiness Packet (AI governance)
    - Trust Center (public posture)
    - Control references included in the Evidence Bundle
  - Next steps: Let us know if you need a sample audit export for your workspace or a brief walkthrough of disable/enable procedures.
  - Thank you, [Your Name]

## 3) AI Governance Clarification (Kill Switch, Audits, Rate Limits)

- **Subject:** Clarification on AI governance controls (disablement, audit, rate limits)
- **Body:**
  - Hi [Name],
  - Addressing your questions on AI controls:
    - Kill switches: Platform-wide and per-workspace, server-enforced (see AI Governance Overview)
    - Audits: Append-only with integrity hashes; exportable via Audit CSV and Evidence Bundle
    - Rate limits: Workspace/user scoped with explicit retry guidance; alerts on spikes
    - Incident readiness: Runbooks and Tabletop Exercises for disablement and investigation
  - Next steps: We can provide a sample export from your workspace or demonstrate a disable/enable flow in a test environment.
  - Regards, [Your Name]
