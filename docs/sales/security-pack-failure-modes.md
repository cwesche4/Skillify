# Security Pack Failure Modes — Sales & CS Playbook (Internal)

Conservative, defensive posture. No workarounds, no future promises.

## Scenarios

### Approval Denied

- **Say:** “This request was denied per our approval process. Approvals are required before delivery and are recorded in the audit log.”
- **Do NOT say:** “We can push it through” / “We’ll fix it later.”
- **Escalate:** Only if process was not followed; involve Compliance.
- **Owner:** Approver/Compliance.

### Entitlement Expired

- **Say:** “Access ended when the entitlement expired per contract terms. We cannot deliver without an active entitlement.”
- **Do NOT say:** “We can extend it informally” / “We’ll backdate access.”
- **Escalate:** To Legal/Compliance if contract renewal/amendment is requested.
- **Owner:** Legal/Compliance.

### Legal Hold

- **Say:** “A legal hold is in effect. Security Pack actions are paused until the hold is cleared.”
- **Do NOT say:** “We can proceed anyway” / “We’ll ignore the hold.”
- **Escalate:** Legal only.
- **Owner:** Legal.

### Incident Freeze

- **Say:** “Actions are temporarily paused due to an incident response. Audit data remains intact.”
- **Do NOT say:** “We guarantee timelines” / “We’ll bypass controls.”
- **Escalate:** Compliance/Engineering if clarification is needed; no commitments.
- **Owner:** Compliance/Engineering.

### Customer Dissatisfaction

- **Say:** “We understand your concern. Security Pack follows contract-entitled access, required approvals, and append-only audit. We cannot alter controls, but we can review the timeline with you.”
- **Do NOT say:** “We’ll customize controls” / “We can relax requirements.”
- **Escalate:** If dispute on facts; involve Compliance.
- **Owner:** Sales/CS with Compliance support.

### Procurement Pressure

- **Say:** “Security Pack terms are standardized: contract-entitled access, required approvals, no SLAs or custom exports. We cannot commit beyond that.”
- **Do NOT say:** “We’ll add SLAs” / “We’ll customize exports” / “We can toggle self-service.”
- **Escalate:** To Legal/Compliance if they request contractual changes.
- **Owner:** Sales; Legal/Compliance on contract matters.
