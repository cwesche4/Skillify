# Enterprise Readiness Self-Check — Security Pack

Purpose: Let buyers self-qualify before security review. No lead gating or marketing language.

## Yes/No Questions (8–12)

1. Do you accept contract-based entitlements (no self-service toggle) for Security Pack access? (Trust Center: Access Control; Procurement Q&A: Access Control)
2. Are you comfortable with role-gated approvals recorded as audit events (no bypass)? (Trust Center: Approvals; Procurement Q&A: Approval & Governance)
3. Do you accept append-only audit logs (no edits/deletes) and read-only timelines? (Trust Center: Audit Integrity; Procurement Q&A: Audit Logging)
4. Can you work with on-demand exports that are point-in-time snapshots (not live feeds)? (Trust Center: Data Handling; Procurement Q&A: Data Handling)
5. Do you require unauthorized/undelivered download attempts to be concealed (404/403)? (Trust Center: Data Handling; Procurement Q&A: Incident/Access)
6. Are scoped service tokens for automation acceptable (no unscoped automation)? (Trust Center: Automation; Procurement Q&A: Automation)
7. Do you accept no SLAs/no guaranteed approvals for Security Pack delivery? (Trust Center: Non-Guarantees; Procurement Q&A: Non-Guarantees)
8. Is SOC-2 alignment (without certification promises) sufficient for your review? (Trust Center: Non-Guarantees; Procurement Q&A: Compliance)
9. Can you handle entitlements expiring with contract terms (no open-ended exceptions)? (Trust Center: Access; Procurement Q&A: Change Management)
10. Are read-only transparency views (entitlements/history/timelines) sufficient for verification? (Trust Center: Transparency; Procurement Q&A: Customer Transparency)

## Scoring Logic

- “Ready for security review”: All Yes.
- “Needs clarification”: 1–2 No/Unsure responses.
- “Not a fit”: 3+ No/Unsure responses, especially on entitlements/no SLAs/append-only logs.

## Sales Handoff Rules

- Ready: Proceed to security review with Trust Center + procurement questionnaire.
- Needs clarification: SE/Compliance to address specific Nos using Trust Center references; no new promises.
- Not a fit: Politely pause; explain contract-entitlement model and non-SLA posture; do not proceed to packet until clarified.
