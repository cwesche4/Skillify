# Sales Security Review Email Templates

Plain, forwardable templates referencing existing Trust Center and AI governance artifacts.

## 1) Initial Security Review Introduction

- **Subject:** Security review materials for your evaluation
- **Body:**
  - Hi [Name],
  - Sharing our security and AI governance materials for your review. Below are the primary references:
    - Trust Center (public overview)
    - AI Governance Overview (Trust Center page)
    - SOC-2 Readiness Packet (AI Governance)
  - Next steps: Please let us know if you need specific workspace exports or additional evidence.
  - Regards, [Your Name]

## 2) Follow-Up After Security Questionnaire

- **Subject:** Follow-up on your security questionnaire
- **Body:**
  - Hi [Name],
  - Following your questionnaire, here are the supporting references:
    - Trust Center
    - SOC-2 Readiness Packet (AI governance controls)
    - AI Governance Overview (kill switches, audits, rate limits)
  - Next steps: We can schedule a short call to walk through controls or provide workspace-specific exports if needed.
  - Thanks, [Your Name]

## 3) Evidence & Audit Artifacts Delivery

- **Subject:** Requested AI governance evidence attached/linked
- **Body:**

  - As requested, here are the AI governance artifacts:

  - Regards, [Your Name]
