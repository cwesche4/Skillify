# Healthcare Security Review Email Templates (AI Governance)

Compliance-aware templates for healthtech security and vendor risk teams.

## 1) Evidence Delivery

- **Subject:** AI governance evidence for your review
- **Body:**
  - Hi [Name],
  - Providing AI governance materials for your review:
    - Audit CSV Export (workspace-specific)
    - Evidence Bundle (audit CSV + control references)
    - Trust Center and AI Governance Overview
  - Next steps: Confirm receipt. We can supply additional filters or walk through disable/enable steps if needed.
  - Regards, [Your Name]

## 2) Security Questionnaire Follow-Up

- **Subject:** Follow-up on security questionnaire (AI governance)
- **Body:**
  - Hi [Name],
  - In response to your questionnaire, the following references apply:
    - Trust Center (AI governance posture)
    - SOC-2 Readiness Packet (AI controls)
    - Evidence Bundle (audit export + control references)
  - Next steps: If you need workspace-specific exports or a brief call, we can schedule it at your convenience.
  - Thank you, [Your Name]

## 3) AI Governance Clarification

- **Subject:** Clarification on AI governance controls
- **Body:**
  - Hi [Name],
  - Addressing your questions on AI controls:
    - Kill switches: Platform and workspace disablement, server-enforced (see AI Governance Overview)
    - Audits: Append-only with integrity hashes; exportable via Audit CSV/Evidence Bundle
    - Rate limits: Workspace/user scoped with retry guidance; alerts on spikes
    - Incident readiness: Runbooks and Tabletop Exercises for disablement and investigation
  - Next steps: We can demonstrate a disable/enable flow in a test workspace or provide a sample audit export.
  - Regards, [Your Name]
