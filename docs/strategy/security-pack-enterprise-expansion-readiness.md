# Enterprise Expansion Readiness — Security Pack

Pure readiness assessment; no roadmap promises or implied commitments.

## Expansion Asks & Coverage

| Ask                       | Coverage          | Safe Response                                                                                        | Requires New Contract?                 |
| ------------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| HIPAA                     | Out of Scope      | “Security Pack is not positioned for HIPAA; no PHI commitments are made.”                            | Yes                                    |
| ISO 27001 evidence        | Partially Covered | “We provide SOC-2-aligned controls and evidence; ISO-specific certification is not provided.”        | If certification/controls are required |
| FedRAMP-lite              | Out of Scope      | “FedRAMP is not in scope for Security Pack; current controls are contract-entitled and append-only.” | Yes                                    |
| Custom SLAs               | Out of Scope      | “Security Pack does not include SLAs or timelines.”                                                  | Yes                                    |
| Custom exports/formats    | Out of Scope      | “Exports are standardized snapshots; no custom formats are offered.”                                 | Yes                                    |
| Additional approval roles | Partially Covered | “Custom approval roles are available only if contractually granted; otherwise not included.”         | If not in contract                     |
| Extra audit fields        | Out of Scope      | “Audit records are append-only and metadata-only; custom fields are not added.”                      | Yes                                    |

## Safe Response Language

- “Security Pack provides contract-entitled access, append-only audit, and read-only exports; additional certifications/SLAs are not included.”
- “Requests beyond current scope would require new contract terms and are not committed.”
- “No roadmap or future commitments are offered.”

## Thresholds (Requires New Contract)

- Any certification (HIPAA, ISO, FedRAMP) beyond current SOC-2 alignment.
- Any SLA, custom export/format, or new audit fields.
- Any entitlements/roles not explicitly granted in contract.
