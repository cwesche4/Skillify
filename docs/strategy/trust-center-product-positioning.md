# Trust Center as a Product Capability — Security Pack

Governance-first positioning; no feature creep or roadmap promises.

## What Trust Center Proves

- Access is contract-entitled, server-enforced; no self-service toggles.
- Approvals are role-gated and append-only; delivery gated by approval + entitlement.
- Audit is append-only and metadata-only; timelines are read-only.
- Downloads are concealed when unauthorized/undelivered; URLs not stored; exports are snapshots.
- Automation is scoped and idempotent; tokens are revocable/rotatable.
- Explicit non-guarantees: no SLAs, no promised approvals, no certification claims.

## What It Intentionally Avoids

- No pricing, SLAs, or roadmap statements.
- No exposure of internal endpoints, schemas, or implementation details.
- No promises of custom controls, exports, or self-service activation.
- No certification guarantees; SOC-2 alignment only.

## Differentiation (Non-Marketing)

- Governance-first: contract entitlements vs SKU toggles or self-service switches.
- Evidence integrity: append-only, metadata-only timelines; concealment for unauthorized/undelivered access.
- Transparency with guardrails: public Trust Center plus gated compliance packet, preventing oversharing while enabling audits.
- Operational discipline: defined lifecycle, entitlement enforcement, and documented non-guarantees reduce legal/audit risk.

## How It Compounds Sales + Audit Trust

- Standard answers reduce procurement cycles and objection handling.
- Consistent, public-safe language lowers audit friction and avoids rework.
- Contract-entitled model aligns sales promises with enforcement, minimizing renegotiation and risk.
- Append-only audits and transparency exports provide reusable evidence for auditors without custom builds.

## Internal Alignment Notes

- Trust Center is the canonical source for public claims; all sales/compliance artifacts must align to it.
- No new claims without lifecycle approval (Draft → Reviewed → Approved → Published).
- Compliance/legal own approvals for changes; sales enablement distributes but does not alter language.
