# Founder Detachment Readiness — Security Pack Trust

Objective: Enterprise deals, audits, and renewals proceed without founder involvement. No tribal knowledge; system-owned trust.

## Founder Dependency Checklist

- [ ] Any answers currently requiring “ask the founder” identified.
- [ ] All Security Pack claims match Trust Center and compliance artifacts.
- [ ] Sales objection responses sourced from objection handling doc, not ad-hoc.
- [ ] Procurement answers sourced from final questionnaire, not founder memory.
- [ ] Audit walkthrough uses SOC-2 interview simulation/script, not founder-led.
- [ ] Contract/entitlement mapping documented; no founder-only interpretations.
- [ ] Escalation rules documented (Legal/Compliance ownership).

## Replacement Artifacts

- Trust Center copy (public) for access, approvals, audit, data handling, non-guarantees.
- Procurement questionnaire (final) for standardized responses.
- Objection handling and battlecards for sales scripts.
- SOC-2 interview simulation and readiness scorecard for audit calls.
- Contract-to-entitlement mapping for legal/commercial clarity.
- Trust Ops governance (lifecycle + RACI) to manage changes.

## Escalation Boundaries

- Founder escalation only if: material misrepresentation risk, contract term creation beyond approved patterns, or existential incident response.
- All other escalations follow role owners: Legal (contracts/exceptions), Compliance (evidence/controls), Sales Enablement (messaging), Engineering (enforcement).
- Default rule: if not a high-risk exception, use documented artifacts and owners; do not route to founder.
