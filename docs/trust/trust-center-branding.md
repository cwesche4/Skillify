# Trust Center Branding & Naming Guide

## 1) Top-Level Name

- **Primary name:** Trust Center
- **Descriptor:** Security, compliance, and AI governance information for enterprise customers.

## 2) Navigation Labels

- **Main nav:** Trust
- **Sub-navigation:** AI Governance, Security Controls, Compliance, Audit & Evidence, Incident Response, Customer Controls.

## 3) Positioning Statement

- The Trust Center provides clear, factual information about how security and AI governance are enforced. It is built for buyers, security teams, and auditors who need transparency and evidence. It is not a marketing page or a roadmap—only current, implemented controls are described.

## 4) Consistency Guidelines

- **Tone:** Neutral, factual, enterprise-focused. Avoid hype or sales language.
- **Language to avoid:** Promises, guarantees, future commitments, jargon-heavy technical detail.
- **References:** Sales and support should point customers to the Trust Center for current controls, evidence exports, and governance summaries; avoid improvising beyond published content.
