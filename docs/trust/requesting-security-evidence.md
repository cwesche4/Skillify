# Requesting Security Evidence (AI Governance)

This page explains what evidence is available and how to request it. No timelines or guarantees are promised.

## Available Evidence

- Audit CSV export (workspace-specific)
- Evidence bundle (audit CSV + control references)
- SOC-2 AI governance readiness packet
- Trust Center and AI Governance Overview (public)
- Runbooks and tabletop exercises (AI incidents)

## How to Request

- Contact your account team or support.
- Provide workspace ID (for workspace-specific exports), requested artifacts, and any filters (date range, action types).
- Confirm NDA status for workspace exports and bundles.

## NDA Requirements

- Workspace-specific exports and evidence bundles require NDA and confirmation of authorized recipients.
- Public Trust Center materials are available without NDA.

## Response Expectations

- Requests are handled as quickly as possible; no specific timelines are guaranteed.
- Some artifacts (workspace exports) may require validation of requester authorization.
