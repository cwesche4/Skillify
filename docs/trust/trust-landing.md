# Trust

## Headline and Subheadline

Secure, controlled, and auditable by design. AI-assisted actions stay inside your workspace and follow server-enforced safeguards.

## What the Platform Protects

Customer data remains within account boundaries. AI actions run only in the requesting workspace and are tied to that workspace’s users—no cross-tenant execution.

## How AI Is Governed

All AI actions are validated on the server. A platform-wide switch and a per-workspace toggle can stop AI immediately, returning clear errors instead of making changes. Every action or denial is logged with before/after snapshots and integrity hashes, and undo requests check for conflicts before applying.

## Security and Operational Controls

Workspace- and user-level rate limits prevent abuse and return retry guidance. Structured metrics and threshold alerts surface spikes in denials, rate limits, or undo conflicts. Runbooks and tabletop exercises support incident response, and AI can be halted instantly if needed. Audit records are exportable, and evidence bundles consolidate audits with control references.

## Transparency and Evidence

Audit exports provide immutable CSV snapshots with metadata. Evidence bundles include audits, control references, and links to governance documentation. SOC-2 readiness materials are available for auditors and procurement teams.

## Customer Control and Assurance

Workspace admins can enable or disable AI actions, export audits, and review activity at any time. Platform operators can disable AI globally when required. Controls are enforced on the server; operational signals exclude payload contents.
