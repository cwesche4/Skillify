# Trust Center Linkage Across Enterprise Workflows — Security Pack

Trust Center is the public source of truth; compliance packet is secondary and gated.

## Touchpoints

### 1) Security Questionnaires

- **When to link:** With initial questionnaire response to provide baseline governance info.
- **Safe language:** “For our public security posture and governance controls, see our Trust Center: [link].”
- **When NOT to link:** If the portal forbids external links; instead, paste the same public-safe copy inline.
- **Escalation:** If detailed evidence is requested, send the final procurement questionnaire first; compliance packet only on request via Compliance.

### 2) SOC-2 Interview Follow-ups

- **When to link:** In written follow-ups to clarify access, approvals, audit integrity, and non-guarantees.
- **Safe language:** “For our public description of access control, approvals, and audit integrity, refer to our Trust Center: [link].”
- **When NOT to link:** During live calls as a substitute for answers; provide verbal answers and follow with the link.
- **Escalation:** If auditors request artifacts beyond the Trust Center, involve Compliance and use the SOC-2 readiness/evidence set.

### 3) Procurement Portals

- **When to link:** In the general security posture section or where allowed for public references.
- **Safe language:** “Our Security Pack governance overview is available on our Trust Center: [link].”
- **When NOT to link:** If the portal disallows external URLs; use the same text without a hyperlink.
- **Escalation:** If the portal asks for documents, attach the final procurement questionnaire; share the compliance packet only if explicitly requested and allowed.

### 4) Deal Rooms / Shared Folders

- **When to link:** In a readme/intro note for security/compliance materials.
- **Safe language:** “Public overview: Trust Center [link]. For detailed Q&A, see the attached procurement questionnaire. Compliance packet available on request.”
- **When NOT to link:** Do not include internal or gated links without context; do not place compliance packet by default.
- **Escalation:** Provide compliance packet only when requested by security/procurement and approved by Compliance.

## Decision Tree (Trust Center vs Compliance Packet)

- Start with Trust Center in all touchpoints.
- If buyer requests detailed answers → send final procurement questionnaire.
- If buyer/auditor explicitly requests deeper evidence → involve Compliance and send compliance packet/readiness docs.
- Do not share internal artifacts or gated content without explicit request and approval.

## “Never Say This” Warnings

- “We will customize controls/exports for you.”
- “We guarantee approval/timelines/SLAs.”
- “Here’s our internal doc/link.”
- “We can turn this on via a toggle.”
