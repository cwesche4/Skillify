## Pass/Fail Confidence Notes

- High confidence for entitlement enforcement, audit immutability, and automation token controls (contract-entitlement resolver, append-only audit, scoped tokens).
- High confidence for transparency (read-only entitlements/timelines) and concealment (404/403 as configured).
- Medium confidence where alerts are optional; alerting is configurable but not mandated in this packet.

## Gaps (Explicit)

- Automated alerting on entitlement anomalies may depend on deployment-specific logging/alert sinks (not included as a hard requirement here).
- Retention durations depend on organizational policy (documented separately); enforcement is not codified in schema.
