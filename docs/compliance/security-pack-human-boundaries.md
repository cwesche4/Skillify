# Security Pack Human Judgment Boundaries

Decisions that must remain manual and require human oversight.

- **Legal approvals:** NDA validation and any contractual constraints before sending workspace-specific exports or evidence bundles.
- **Non-standard evidence requests:** Any artifact outside the approved bundles/exports must be reviewed by Security/GRC (and Legal if needed).
- **Incident overrides:** Emergency disablement or expedited evidence sharing during an incident requires documented human approval and post-incident review.
