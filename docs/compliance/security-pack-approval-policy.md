# Security Pack Approval Policy

Defines who may initiate and approve sending security evidence. This is a process policy only; no automated enforcement is included.

## Roles

- **Initiators:** Sales, Support, Success, or Solutions engineers.
- **Approvers:** Security/GRC; Legal when NDA or contractual terms apply; Engineering only for technical clarifications.

## Initiate vs Approve

- Initiators prepare the package (template, artifacts, trust links) and request approval.
- Approvers must review and approve before send when NDA or workspace-scoped evidence is included.
- Public Trust Center links and non-sensitive docs may be sent by initiators without approval if pre-cleared by Security/GRC.

## Dual Approval

- Required when: workspace-specific exports (audit CSV), evidence bundles marked NDA, or SOC-2 escalations.
- One approver from Security/GRC; Legal approval if NDA or contractual restrictions apply.

## Legal / Security Sign-off

- NDA artifacts and customer-specific exports require Security/GRC approval and Legal confirmation of NDA status.
- SOC-2/GRC escalations require Security/GRC approval.

## Emergency Override

- Allowed only for incident communications when time-critical.
- Must be documented with reason, initiator, approver (if available), timestamp, and artifacts sent.
- Post-incident review required to confirm appropriateness and log completeness.

## Logging

- Record initiator, approver(s), timestamp, recipient, artifacts sent, NDA status, workspace scope, and reason if override used.
